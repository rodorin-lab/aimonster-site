## Scope and safety

Discovery covers the inspected development directories and the GitHub candidates returned in this session; it is not a forensic scan of every file on every disk. Unidentified titles such as YO-KAI SLASHER and NEON RIFT are not represented as playable until their actual entry points are identified.

Preview is separate from production. Checkout, webhook, pack data, licensing and original creature assets are unchanged. No real payment is made in testing. VR immersion, cross-device saves and game-clear achievements are not implemented. Passport stamps record opening a game, not clearing it.
